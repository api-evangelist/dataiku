<p><span>Every time you type a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or any other LLM-powered service, you're sending data to an external server. For casual questions, that's fine. But in enterprise settings, those prompts often contain customer names, email addresses, social security numbers, medical records, financial details, and internal business data that should never leave your environment.</span></p>
