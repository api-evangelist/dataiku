This article evaluates Alteryx's position in the 2026 analytics market, noting that while it remains strong for drag-and-drop data preparation, it faces limitations in cloud-native architecture and AI capabilities. Governance and compliance mandates including the EU AI Act now require audit trails, lineage tracking, and role-based controls that reshape enterprise requirements. The analysis presents six alternative platforms including Dataiku, Power BI, Tableau, Qlik, ThoughtSpot, and Looker, each addressing different organizational needs across the analytics-to-AI lifecycle.
