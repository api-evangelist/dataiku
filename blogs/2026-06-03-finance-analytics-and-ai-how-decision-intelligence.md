This article explores how AI-powered decision intelligence platforms transform financial operations by moving beyond historical reporting to predictive decision-making. The piece covers applications in FP&A (cash flow forecasting, driver-based budgeting, expense anomaly detection), risk management (credit scoring, fraud detection, AML), and financial reporting, while emphasizing the importance of governance frameworks like SR 26-2 and EU AI Act compliance for finance teams implementing these technologies.
