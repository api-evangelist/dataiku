The bottleneck in most enterprise AI programs is not model accuracy, and it is not the quality of GenAI outputs. It is the last mile: getting a prediction, a recommendation, or an agent's reasoning into an actual operational decision, at speed, with a clear accountability trail. Enterprises are building more AI than ever.
